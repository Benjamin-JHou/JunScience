# Academic Scientific Manuscript & Journal Formatting

## Overview
Transforms synthesized scientific findings, trial statistics, and molecular analyses into formal academic manuscript drafts formatted according to specific target journal guidelines (e.g. *Journal of Hepatology*, *Hepatology*, *Nature Medicine*, *Frontiers in Immunology*).

## Structured Manuscript Architecture
1. **Title & Running Title**: Concise, informative, $< 120$ characters.
2. **Structured Abstract**: Background, Methods, Results (with quantitative effect sizes and 95% CIs), Conclusions ($< 250-300$ words).
3. **Keywords**: 4-6 MeSH / medical index terms.
4. **Introduction**: Clinical problem significance, unresolved mechanistic bottleneck, rationale and hypothesis.
5. **Results**: Sequenced subsections with statistical tables, figures, and verified evidence anchors.
6. **Discussion**: Key findings interpretation, biological mechanism, clinical implications, study limitations.
7. **Methods**: Detailed experimental, computational, and statistical procedures.
8. **Declarations & References**: Ethical approvals, data availability statement, AMA/NLM standard citation formatting.
